# Swiggy Restaurant & Sales Analysis | SQL Server

### Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Swiggy restaurant and sales data using Microsoft SQL Server to evaluate restaurant performance, customer ratings, food pricing, sales trends, and geographic market distribution.

The objective is to extract meaningful insights from restaurant and order data to understand business performance and identify potential growth opportunities.

### Business Problems Solved

The analysis addresses the following business questions:

#### Restaurant Performance: 

Identify top-rated restaurants and evaluate restaurant performance across different locations.

#### Customer Ratings:

Analyse customer ratings and rating counts to understand restaurant popularity and customer satisfaction.

#### Food Pricing Analysis: 

Examine food prices across restaurants, categories, and locations.

#### Sales Analysis: 

Evaluate monthly sales growth and identify changes in sales performance.

#### Geographic Analysis: 

Identify top-performing restaurants across different states and analyse location-wise sales trends.

#### Revenue Contribution:

Calculate monthly and geographic contributions to overall sales.

#### Market Analysis: 

Identify potential markets and understand regional business performance.

### Tools & Technologies

Database: Microsoft SQL Server

Language: SQL

### SQL Skills Demonstrated

Data exploration and analysis

Multi-table JOIN operations

Aggregate functions (SUM, COUNT, AVG)

Common Table Expressions (CTEs)

Subqueries

Window Functions (DENSE_RANK, RANK)

GROUP BY and HAVING

Date functions and monthly trend analysis

Ranking and percentage contribution calculations

### Key Insights

#### Top Performing Restaurant:

Natural Ice Cream Restaurant of New Delhi emerged as highest-rated restaurants.

#### Monthly sales growth:

Just two months February and June showing decline in sales, Rest months shows promising growth.

#### Geographic sales contribution:

Karnataka recorded highest revenue contribution showing large market potential.  

#### Restaurant Coverage:

Rajasthan with highest number of working restaurants favors business expansion.

### Business Recommendations

Restaurant Strategy: Focus on high-performing and highly rated restaurants to strengthen customer trust.

Pricing Strategy: Evaluate food pricing across locations to maintain competitive pricing.

Customer Strategy: Use customer rating patterns to identify areas where restaurant service and food quality can improve.

Market Expansion: Focus on regions showing strong sales performance and promising market opportunities.

Sales Strategy: Monitor monthly sales trends to identify growth opportunities and address declining performance.

### Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL Server can be used to analyse restaurant and sales data, solve real-world business problems, and generate actionable insights. It highlights the importance of data-driven decision-making in understanding restaurant performance, customer behaviour, pricing strategies, and geographic market trends.
